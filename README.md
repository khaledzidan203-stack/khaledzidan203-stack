## Portfolio Principles

- Validate the **grain and keys before joining**
- Treat **NULL, blank and zero differently**
- Prefer **ratio of totals** when aggregating ratios unless the KPI contract states otherwise
- Keep business logic governed and reusable
- Reconcile important KPIs independently
- Separate **descriptive findings** from causal claims
- Build dashboards around **decisions**, not decoration
